CORE SERVICES & SOLUTIONS

📐 1. Kitchen Engineering & Layout Optimization

  • Space Optimization: Custom floorplan redesigns for hot kitchens, artisan pizzerias, fast-casual formats, and beverage bars.
  • Lean Hardware Strategy (CAPEX): Identifying and stripping out redundant, overpriced equipment pushed by equipment vendors to slash initial CapEx and utility overhead.

  • Standardized Recipe Specs: Implementing strict yield guides, portion specs, and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
  • Brigade Training: Practical hands-on training for kitchen teams to eliminate food waste and streamline prep routines.
